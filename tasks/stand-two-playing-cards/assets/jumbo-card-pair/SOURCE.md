Original procedural geometry for this experiment. Two 89 × 127 × 0.65 mm rigid laminated jumbo cards, each modeled at 8 g. The edge profile, stiffness, mass and Coulomb friction are simulation assumptions. Printed marks have no collision. The starting rack slots are outside the freestanding work area.

Friction assumption for this release: card static 0.75, mat static 0.80, dynamic 90% of static. These model a textured finish and require checking with a physical pair for transfer.
