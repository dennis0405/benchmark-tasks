## Commercial reference and access

Use a new **nVent HOFFMAN ASE4X4X3** Type 1 painted steel enclosure, with knockouts. The NK suffix denotes a different blank product. Use the C member of the B–C pair on the target side. The catalog shows B–C on all four side walls, a removable screw cover, a solid mounting back, a 4 × 4 × 3 in body (101.6 × 101.6 × 76.2 mm), and 16 gauge body and cover. C is 3/4–1 in conduit trade size. The drawing is viewed from outside. [HOFFMAN Spec-00258, product table and pattern drawing](https://www.nvent.com/sites/default/files/acquiadam/assets/Spec-00258.pdf) · [HOFFMAN pocket reference](https://www.nvent.com/sites/default/files/acquiadam/assets/HOFFMAN-CAT-Cat00105-CommPocket-EN-2110_0.pdf)

Greenlee gives approximately 28.3 and 34.6 mm as finished punch diameters for these two trade sizes. These are nominal aperture references. HOFFMAN's drawing gives no production tolerance for the web, tie count, clocking, shear strength or fracture load for this SKU. The simulated single tie at 6 o'clock, nominal 1.52 mm gauge conversion and collision kerf are modeling assumptions. Inspect or measure an unworked sample and photograph both faces before claiming a match. [Greenlee 3/4 in](https://www.greenlee.com/mx/en/punch-3-4-cond-speed-pop7212sp-3-4p) · [Greenlee 1 in](https://www.greenlee.com/ca/en/punch-1-cond-speed-pop-7212sp-1p-7212sp-1p)

The old 63 mm disk and straight push from behind the plate do not fit this workpiece. In the bench configuration the mounting back is down and cover removed; between opposite side walls there is about 99 mm clear before manufacturing features. The former rear approach crosses the opposite solid wall. A hand may reach down through the cover opening, a different path requiring a turn. The new demo uses the exterior approach with a rigidly mounted, 5.5 × 1.2 mm flat blade. The other arm stays clear. The simulation includes all four side walls, a bottom, and static markings for other knockouts. It omits cover lips, screws and the mechanical behavior of unselected knockouts. Two-sided reachability of a real YAM has not been physically measured.

Square D instructs an inward screwdriver strike away from the tie and rocking the center loose. Its instructions also describe screwdriver/pliers for removing larger rings. This is separate evidence from a different manufacturer. A model-specific HOFFMAN-approved removal tool and a measured load for ASE4X4X3 were not located. Robot blade travel is controlled pressing, with no calibrated hammer impact. ## Physical reset and adjudication

Use an empty, never-energized enclosure before wiring. Secure the back to the same raised bench block with the target side facing the working arm. Use one factory-intact C knockout for every trial. Prefer a fresh enclosure with the same part and lot and the same selected side for each trial. Keep the cover off in every trial, verify the new part is flush and attached, and record side, tie location, sheet thickness, opening reference, tool protrusion and fixture orientation. Do not reinstall previously broken slugs. If the stamping or tab position differs, flag the specimen before the trial. Use paint dots on both slug faces and a pair of witness marks next to the ligament, one on the slug and one on the annulus. The simulated dot has diameter 11 mm and does not change contact geometry.

Record a frontal inspection view and an oblique/overhead view with a scale, ensuring both hands and blade are visible at the end. For the bend, project both nearby witness marks to the common tie line using the initial image as the reference. Tilt above 14°, tie-line misregistration below 4 mm and continuing overlap indicate partial credit only if the slug remains attached at trial end. The exposed marks on the two faces are offset on either side of the tie; measuring raw center-to-center paint distance would include the initial offset. For full credit show the separate disk, unobstructed smaller entry, ring still registered to its frame and both hands/tool outside a 72 mm exclusion radius for 1 s. Score through the end of the trial. A previously bent but now detached slug that still blocks the passage receives zero. The simulator compares the projected disk silhouette with the opening and requires a visible 2 mm gap. If the ring moves by more than 2 mm or tilts by more than 8°, cancel prior credit. Inspect ring retention after each trial, even if the ring is flush. The simulator uses the outer fracture record as the counterpart of this check. A failed retention check cancels all credit, even when the pose looks normal. Pause adjudication on occlusion instead of assuming hidden ties are intact. A separated ring lodged flush may require a gentle post-trial retention check; pose alone cannot certify that hidden bond.

For ambiguous openings, after the scored interval insert a smooth 27.0 mm GO cylinder coaxially from the working side, 60 mm past the panel or to the specified clear depth. A 32.0 mm NO-GO should not enter the smaller opening. Never force either gauge; inspect the full circumference and the separate slug as well. These are proposed bench gauges for the 28.3/34.6 mm simulation reference. Measure a spare reference unit first. A gauge is a cross-check: an obstructed frontal passage or damaged ring cannot earn full credit. Hardware gauge pass/fail, actual break force and real robot execution have not been tested.
