Original compound primitives and dimensions for this task. The balance operation is inspired by DJI's Ronin 4D manual, tilt-axis depth adjustment (https://dl.djicdn.com/downloads/DJI_Ronin_4D/20220216/DJI_Ronin_4D_User_Manual_v1.2_enI.pdf). The one-axis fixture and its spring brake are an original design; no manufacturer meshes are included. build_asset.py generates gimbal.usda from gimbal.xml. The MuJoCo file is also the CPU passive reference model.
