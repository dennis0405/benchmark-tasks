# Landing-gear asset

Original simplified rigid-body model of a two-speed trailer leg with a grounded start, nominal 305 mm travel, and passive keeper. The 355.6 mm-radius crank uses JOST A400 gearing. The crank sweeps in the aisle beside the robot supports. The gearbox axis is at (0.744628, −0.260, 0.910) m, yaw 180°. The deployed crank starts at 35° and the keeper is aligned with the 90° shaft position. The selector stroke is 28 mm.

The model uses one telescoping carriage and a 230 × 210 × 26 mm shoe on a fixed 380 × 380 × 20 mm pad. The shoe is simplified from the reference set's 10 × 10 in sand shoe. A gravity-compensated 40 kg effective carriage, a compliant screw and measured pad reaction generate the bench load. Initial screw preload is 28 µm, stiffness is 1.2 MN/m and damping is 4,800 N·s/m. The observed initial pad reaction is 33.4 N. These are simulator parameters. The README gives the matched hardware set, preload/reset method and external score checks. Physical torque and drive loads have not been validated on stock equipment.
