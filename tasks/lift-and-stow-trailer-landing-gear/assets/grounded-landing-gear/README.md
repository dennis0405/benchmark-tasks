Derived from the locally authored landing-finish fixture. The 355.6 mm crank and reduced leads of 25.4/32 and 25.4/3.5 mm per turn are preserved. A telescoping inner leg terminates on a colliding 230 × 210 mm shoe. A fixed 20 mm steel plate rests flush on the station floor at −12.5 mm. The nominal initial clearance is zero. The fixture tests the change of load, shift and parking over a short stroke. It does not represent the full ground-to-road clearance of a highway trailer.
