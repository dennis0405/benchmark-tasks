# Open snack pouch redesign — experimental

This is an isolated geometry and material research prototype, not the Arena
scene, scorer, clip, or YAM demonstration. The previous video showed a directly
initialized fold in an elastic surface; it did not demonstrate a physical
folding action or a calibrated snack packet.

## Geometry

`pouch_geometry.py` generates two connected film surfaces with shared vertices
on the two sides and bottom. Only the torn top is a boundary. The nominal
20 x 16 cm body has a 44 mm maximum thickness, tapering to a 4 mm open mouth.
These are provisional design dimensions, not measurements of a real packet.
The shape is merely a bulged stress-free rest geometry: it does not simulate
sealed air or loose chips. The top edge is a straight cut and the seals have no
finite width. It is not a textured or submission-ready asset.

`test_pouch_geometry.py` checks the boundary, manifold edges, triangle area,
dimensions and the two-surface connectivity. It does not check dynamic contact.

## Preliminary free-body control

Using the Newton VBD environment, `pouch_body_probe.py` instantiates this mesh
on a floor with no clip, contents, air, robot, or direct fold initialization.
It uses the prior experimental triangle and bending coefficients, not measured
material parameters. Its source is an adapted copy of the prior Newton probe;
only the default flat geometry without clip is supported here.

A 0.5 s run with 80 x 64 cells per face remained finite. Its total height went
from 44 mm at the first step to 11 mm at 0.5 s as the unsupported body collapsed.
The saved intersection checker found zero strict crossings at 0.004, 0.25, and
0.5 s. Sampling is sparse and cannot prove continuous collision freedom, and
this is not a folding, grip, sealing or retention test.

```sh
source /data/IsaacLab-Arena/yam.env
cd /data/benchmark-tasks/tasks/close-snack-bag
python -m unittest -v test_pouch_geometry
python pouch_body_probe.py /data/snack-runs/pouch-body-new --duration 0.5
python check_surface_intersections.py /data/snack-runs/pouch-body-new/surface-snapshots.npz
```

## Design and validation still required

- Model a partially filled bag with movable content or a justified compliant
  surrogate and account for escaped air; a fixed box is not equivalent.
- Model the multilayer film's stretch, bending, damping and irreversible crease
  response. Current elastic rest-shape bending cannot establish real crease
  behavior. Determine supported solver capabilities before choosing a model.
- Measure a real example: dimensions and film thickness, mass, fold/release
  angle versus time, force versus displacement, and clip force/opening. Fit
  model parameters to those observations and keep independent validation trials.
- Demonstrate finger-driven fold, clip contact, release for 3 seconds, and check
  all film/film and film/clip intersections, retention and leakage proxies.
  Validate scoring against those observations before integrating YAM.

The snack-film description is informed by [market-available snack packaging
research](https://doi.org/10.1002/cite.202300110). Newton's surface bending and
contact parameters are described in its [VBD tuning documentation](https://newton-physics.github.io/newton/stable/concepts/simulation_tuning_vbd.html);
those parameter names alone do not validate any material calibration.

## Movable compliant-contents and fold experiments

`pouch_contents_probe.py` adds one freely moving, deformable cuboid inside the
packet, as a preliminary compliant support surrogate. It is neither separate
chips nor an air model. Its provisional rest size is 70 x 60 x 18 mm, density
100 kg/m^3, Lame parameters 100,000 Pa, damping 10 Pa s. These numbers are not
fit to real contents. The cuboid is generated by the installed Isaac Lab mesher;
node count changed across runs (14--21), so exact reproducibility needs further
investigation. Simulation uses the original uncalibrated film material.

In a single 3 s **unfolded** run, the packet height changed from 44.0 mm to
approximately 18.4 mm and remained finite; 15 contents nodes were inside the
film at each of the six saved times (0.004, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3 s). No strict
film self-crossings were found in those same samples. The node check uses
vertical intersections with the actual film triangles. It only checks contents
vertices; it does not test edges/faces, fluid leakage, forces, or continuous time.
Compare with the previous no-contents control: 11 mm height at 0.5 s. The
controls were separate trials, not calibrated material measurements.

`--foldable-mouth` tapers the last 60 mm toward a thin mouth; `--folded`
directly initializes a two-fold pose and is **not** a physical fold action.
Its initial mesh had no detected intersections. In the first 1 s folded run,
the fold-reversal heuristic dropped from 100% at the first step to 0% at
0.25 s, while film self-crossings were 0, 0, 2, 3 at 0.004, 0.25, 0.5, 1 s.
The contained nodes stayed inside at all sampled times. This is a failed
collision/retention control, not a successful fold or sealing demo.

The solver uses edge rest angles in its bending term. Updating those angles
could be investigated as a crease approximation, but the current code does not
implement or validate plastic deformation. No inference about actual packet
fold recovery can be made without calibration. Before a YAM demo, resolve
folded self-contact, validate the coupled dynamic clip and its force, develop
and validate a crease model, and test real folding and release.

```sh
python pouch_contents_probe.py /data/snack-runs/content-check --duration 3 \
  --contents-stiffness 100000 --contents-damping 10
python check_surface_intersections.py /data/snack-runs/content-check/surface-snapshots.npz
python inspect_pouch_contents.py /data/snack-runs/content-check/surface-snapshots.npz
python pouch_contents_probe.py /data/snack-runs/fold-check --folded --foldable-mouth \
  --duration 1 --contents-stiffness 100000 --contents-damping 10
```

A second folded trial used 8 substeps, 30 VBD iterations, a 0.2 mm self-contact
radius, 0.4 mm self-contact margin and a collision check every substep. At the
four saved times (0.004, 0.25, 0.5 and 1 s), no strict surface crossings were
detected and all 18 contents nodes were inside. The fold heuristic again fell
from 100% to 0% by 0.25 s. This is a promising collision setting, but a single
sampled trial does not establish continuous nonpenetration or realistic crease
behavior. The simulation took roughly 100 s on the L40S for 1 s simulated.

```sh
python pouch_contents_probe.py /data/snack-runs/fold-check-finer --folded --foldable-mouth \
  --duration 1 --contents-stiffness 100000 --contents-damping 10 \
  --substeps 8 --iterations 30 --self-radius .0002 --self-margin .0004 --collision-interval 1
```
